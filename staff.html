STAFF STRUCTURE

Two distinct paths.

Moderation and technical staff serve different purposes and follow separate appointment systems.

MODERATION PATH

Trainee → Helper → Moderator → Senior Moderator → Admin

Moderation staff progress through experience, activity, reliability, and demonstrated judgment.

TECHNICAL PATH

Engineer

Engineer is a separate specialist role and does not require progression through the moderation team.
