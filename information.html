AXLOMC NETWORK INFORMATION

A network built to stay fresh.

AxloMC is a planned Minecraft network that combines a wide selection of game modes, connected progression, community-driven events, cosmetics, ranks, guilds, voting rewards, and seasonal experiences.

THE CORE VISION

Always something new to do.

AxloMC is designed around variety. Players can move between competitive games, long-term Survival progression, creative building, community events, and large special experiences without leaving the same network.

01

VARIETY

Multiple game modes

Different play styles, match lengths, objectives, and progression systems reduce repetition and give players new activities to discover.

02

CONNECTION

Shared network identity

Cosmetics, ranks, currencies, events, guilds, voting, and achievements connect otherwise separate experiences.

03

COMMUNITY

Player-driven activity

Players do more than join matches. Eligible players can host events, build guilds, send gifts, and contribute to network activity.

GAME MODES

Many ways to play.

Each game mode provides a different experience while contributing to the wider AxloMC network.

S

LONG-TERM WORLD

Survival

Build bases, protect land, create shops, participate in the economy, collect Flakes, and develop a long-term presence.

  • Land protection and player homes
  • Player shops and market systems
  • Survival Kits based on rank
  • Flakes and Survival Lottery
  • Seasonal activities and quests
B

TEAM STRATEGY

BedWars

Defend the team bed, collect resources, purchase upgrades, and eliminate opposing teams.

  • Solo and team matchmaking
  • Daily and weekly quests
  • Special rank quests
  • Experience and level progression
  • Network currency rewards
S

FAST BATTLES

SkyWars

Fight from floating islands, gather equipment, control the center, and become the final surviving player or team.

  • Fast competitive rounds
  • Kill and win progression
  • Daily and weekly quests
  • Mode levels and statistics
  • Network rewards
D

DIRECT COMPETITION

Duels

Challenge other players through a large selection of short, focused competitive formats.

  • Sumo and Boxing
  • Bed Fight and Bridge
  • BuildUHC and Potion
  • Crystal and Sword
  • Additional rotating formats
C

BUILD AND CREATE

Creative

Use personal building spaces to design projects, test ideas, collaborate, and create outside competitive gameplay.

  • Personal Creative realms
  • Rank-based realm capacity
  • Collaborative building
  • Community showcases
  • Creative events
E

COMMUNITY ACTIVITY

Event Hall

A dedicated location where eligible players can organize and host structured community games.

  • Player-hosted activities
  • Automatic network alerts
  • Host control tools
  • Participant management
  • Multiple event formats

SIGNATURE COMMUNITY SYSTEM

Events powered by players.

Event Hall changes the normal relationship between the network and the community. Instead of relying only on staff-hosted events, eligible ranked players can use built-in tools to organize activities for other players.

ECONOMY AND PROGRESSION

Different currencies. Clear purposes.

AxloMC separates gameplay progression from premium purchases so each currency has a clear role.

AX

NETWORK CURRENCY

AX

AX is earned through gameplay, matches, quests, participation, achievements, and selected events.

Armor cosmetics Kill messages Wings Effects Visual rewards Network identity

PREMIUM CURRENCY

Jewels

Jewels are obtained through purchases and are used for ranks, upgrades, Chat Tags, and gifts.

Permanent ranks MVP upgrades Rank gifts Chat Tags Limited items Premium cosmetics

VOTING

Vote and Heart systems

Voting supports network visibility while giving players rewards. Hearts provide additional progress and can connect voting to lotteries, cosmetics, and long-term goals.

SURVIVAL

Flakes and lotteries

Survival can use its own activity systems, collectible Flakes, player economy, and recurring lottery mechanics without replacing the wider network economy.

PROGRESSION

Quests and levels

Daily, weekly, and special quests give players short-term goals, while levels and achievements provide long-term progression.

RANK SYSTEM

Identity, convenience, and community tools.

Permanent ranks provide lasting benefits. King and God are optional monthly upgrades available to players who already own MVP.

COMMUNITY SYSTEMS

More than matchmaking.

Social systems are designed to create connections between game modes, players, groups, and network events.

01

Guilds

Create organized player groups, build a shared identity, play together, and participate in future guild activities.

02

Chat Tags

Collect Common, Epic, Legendary, seasonal, special, and custom tags to personalize chat appearance.

03

Cosmetics

Use wings, armor appearances, effects, messages, and other visual rewards without replacing skill-based gameplay.

04

Museum and history

Preserve important network moments, showcase achievements, and recognize early or notable parts of AxloMC history.

05

Player stories

Community posts, event recaps, featured builds, and accomplishments keep the website connected to activity inside the network.

06

Network progression

Quests, achievements, levels, rewards, gifts, and events give players reasons to return across different game modes.

SPECIAL EXPERIENCES

Events that change the routine.

Seasonal content and large staff-hosted competitions introduce limited experiences beyond normal game modes.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Start small. Build carefully.

AxloMC is currently a concept in the planning phase. The network should grow through complete and testable releases rather than attempting to launch every proposed feature at once.

PHASE 01

Core Foundation

Establish the server infrastructure, lobby, permissions, essential commands, menus, player data, and basic network systems.

Lobby Permissions Player Data Core Menus
PHASE 02

Survival and Economy

Launch a stable Survival experience with protection, homes, player shops, kits, voting, progression, and economy systems.

Survival AX Voting Rank Kits
PHASE 03

Community Activity

Add Event Hall, player hosting tools, event announcements, gifting, cosmetics, guild foundations, and community rewards.

Event Hall Gifting Guilds Cosmetics
PHASE 04

Competitive Expansion

Introduce BedWars, SkyWars, matchmaking, quests, statistics, achievements, levels, and carefully balanced rewards.

BedWars SkyWars Quests Levels
PHASE 05

Long-Term Network Growth

Expand into Duels, Creative, seasonal updates, large tournaments, advanced guild features, and recurring content.

Duels Creative Seasonal Events Major Tournaments
